Privacy Policy

Turf Measurement Optimizer · Maintained by Saliko Solutions · Last updated September 5, 2026

Turf Measurement Optimizer is a measuring and quoting tool for turf installers. This page explains what data the app touches, why, and where it goes. Short version: almost nothing leaves your phone.

No account required

You don't sign up, log in, or create a profile to use this app. There is no user account system.

What the app uses, and why

Camera / AR (LiDAR): Used to measure outdoor areas via ARKit. Camera frames and depth data are processed on your device to calculate measurements — they are not uploaded or stored on any server.

Photo Library: Used only if you choose to import a photo for manual measurement, or save a measurement photo. Photos stay on your device unless you explicitly share/email them yourself.

Location (optional): If you allow it, GPS coordinates are attached to a saved project so you can find the job site again later. Location access is optional — declining it does not block any measurement feature. Location data stays on your device and is only included if you choose to share a project.

What we don't do

  • No account creation, no passwords
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties

In-app purchases

The app offers optional tips to support development, processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit. Payment information is handled by Apple — we never see or store your payment details.

Sharing a quote

If you use the app's email-sharing feature, the email (including any measurement data, photos, or GPS coordinates you choose to include) is composed and sent using your own device's mail client. That data passes through your email provider like any other email you send — we don't have a server in the middle.

Children's privacy

This app is a professional tool for landscaping and turf installation work and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
