Common questions

Why won't the measurement screen open?
Turf Measurement Optimizer requires a device with a LiDAR sensor — iPhone 12 Pro or later (Pro/Pro Max models), or an iPad Pro (2020 or later). Standard iPhones without LiDAR can't run the AR measurement feature.

Do I need an account?
No. Open the app and start measuring — no sign-up required.

Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Measurements, photos, and project data stay on your device unless you choose to email a quote yourself. See the Privacy Policy for details.
